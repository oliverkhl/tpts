Company Background

Our Background & Vision

Two Parrots Trading Solutions Co., Ltd. is a social agribusiness dedicated to building traceable value chains. Backed by 20 years of agri-food experience, we support Cambodian smallholders while delivering export-ready products globally.


Vision: To be a leading, trusted, and ethical agribusiness partner driving sustainable growth for farmers, partners, and consumers.

Our Mission & Strategy

Mission: To build sustainable agricultural value chains delivering high-quality and traceable products while promoting environmental and social responsibility.


Business Model: Direct partnerships with agricultural cooperatives and SMEs, green investments, eco-friendly packaging, and zero-waste initiative models.

Production Line & Quality Control

Processing Facility & Equipment

Our processing setup uses multi-stage automated lines including Mixers, Steamer systems, Flash Fryers, Coolers, and Weight Checkers integrated with Metal Detectors to ensure absolute product safety.

Quality Management System (QMS)

We execute Internal Control System (ICS) monitoring across all raw materials. Our operations follow GMP and HACCP protocols while advancing towards complete FSSC22000 and BRC certifications for international markets.

Strategic Growth Roadmap

2025

Expansion of processing factories for pepper, flours, noodles, and seasoning mixes. Partnering with 6 agricultural cooperatives and 500 smallholders in Cambodia.

2026

Brand scaling and instant noodle factory expansion. Targeting export channels to Japan, Korea, Australia, UK, EU, and the US while integrating 1,000 farmers across 10 cooperatives.

2027 - 2029

Full implementation of digital farm-to-table traceability systems, target partnership of 20 cooperatives, zero-waste operational models, and increasing smallholder farm income by 30%.
