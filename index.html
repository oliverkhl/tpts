Our History & Vision

Established in 2009 and headquartered in Phnom Penh, Two Parrots sources, processes, packages, and distributes Cambodian agricultural products domestically. In 2025, Two Parrots Trading Solutions Co., Ltd. was established to distribute and trade across international markets.


Vision: To be a leading, trusted, and ethical agribusiness partner driving sustainable growth for farmers, partners, and consumers.

Our Mission & Integrated Model

Mission: To build high-quality agricultural value chains delivering traceable products while promoting environmental and social responsibility.


Integrated Supply Chain (Farm to Market): SOURCING (Farmers & Cooperatives) → PROCESSING (Phnom Penh Facility) → QUALITY & TRACEABILITY (ICS Monitored) → FLEXIBLE PACKAGING → DISTRIBUTION (Domestic & Export Markets).

Distribution & Commercial Channels

  • Domestic Networks: 35+ Distributors, 250+ Retail Outlets, 45+ Supermarkets across Cambodia.
  • International Markets: Global distribution partners across Japan, New Zealand, USA, France, and Australia.
  • Target Enterprise Formats: Bulk ingredients, White Label/Private Label, and Branded Retail.

Development Partners & Collaborators

  • Australian CAPRED / Aid: Supporting Tboung Khmum province pepper farmers to secure GLOBALG.A.P. certifications.
  • EU / GIZ: Upgrading Cambodian pepper and cashew value chains to transition smallholders into processed value-added goods.
  • ADB Frontier Program: Enhancing market development and regional supply chain integration.
  • Quality Testing: Product development testing partnerships with international labs (EUROFINS).

Processing Facility & Quality Control System

Facility Layout & Processing Capacity

Located in Phnom Penh, our production plant features automated processing rooms including Raw Material Warehouses, Steam Sterilization Units, Grinding Rooms, Air Blowing Systems, and Dedicated Packaging Areas.


  • Black Pepper Capacity: 3,600 MT / year (Whole & Fine Ground)
  • White Pepper Capacity: 1,800 MT / year (Whole & Fine Ground)
  • Drying & Safety: Traditional sun-drying combined with industrial steam sterilization.

Quality Management System (QMS) & ICS

Supported by internal QA/QC teams and external auditors. We enforce an Internal Control System (ICS) monitoring farm raw materials and factory workflows.


  • Standards Compliance: GMP, HACCP, Global GAP, SRP, Organic standards.
  • Laboratory Testing: Rigorous microbiological, chemical, and physical safety testing.
  • Foreign Matter Target: ≤ 0% foreign matter in finished export lots.

Strategic Growth Roadmap

2020 – 2022

Solidified domestic foundation in Cambodia, selling 250 MT of black peppercorns across nationwide retail networks, supermarkets, and distributors.

2023 – 2025

Expanded domestic pepper sales to 350 MT annually (~70% market share). Launched international trading arm (Two Parrots Trading Solutions Co., Ltd.) and initiated exports to Japan, NZ, USA, France, and Australia while integrating 6 cooperatives and 500 smallholder farmers.

2026

2027 – 2029
